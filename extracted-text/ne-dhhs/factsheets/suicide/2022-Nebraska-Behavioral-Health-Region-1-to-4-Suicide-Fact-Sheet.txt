group, with significantly fewer Hispanic suicide deaths.

In 2022, about 54% of suicides in Ne -
braska Behavioral Health Regions 1 
to 4 occurred in the first half of the 
year. January , June, and September 
saw the highest numbers, accounting 
for 38% of the total. The majority of 
victims during this period were white 
males, averaging 46 years old with at 
least a high school education. In 2021, 
the pattern shifted, with the fourth 
quarter seeing the highest deaths, 
while the first quarter had the fewest.
