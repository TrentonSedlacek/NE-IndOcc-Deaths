Among working age population who 
died by suicide in 2020 and 2021 in 
Nebraska, males were most likely to 
use firearms, and females were most 
likely to die by poisoning (including 
drug overdose). The average age for 
firearm-related deaths was 41, for 
strangulation 38, and for poisoning 42.
