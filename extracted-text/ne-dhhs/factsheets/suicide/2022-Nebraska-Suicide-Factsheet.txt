In 2022, about 26% of suicides in 
Nebraska occurred in the third quar -
ter. The month of June registered the 
highest number of suicides, compris -
ing 11.5% of the total. The predomi -
nant demographic among individuals 
who lost their lives to suicide during 
these months were white males, with 
an average age of 52 and holding a 
high school degree. In 2022, deaths 
were nearly evenly distributed across 
all four quarters, whereas in 2021, the 
fourth quarter had the highest count, 
accounting for nearly 32% of the 
deaths.
