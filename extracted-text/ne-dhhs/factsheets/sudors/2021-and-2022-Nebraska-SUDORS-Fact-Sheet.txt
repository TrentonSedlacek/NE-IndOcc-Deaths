the largest share of drug overdose 
deaths, followed by fentanyl, stimu -
lants, and amphetamine/metham -
phetamine. Among stimulant-relat -
ed overdose deaths, the average age 
at death was 39 years for males and 
42 years for females. Substance in -
volvement was generally similar for 
males and females; however, antide -
pressants were among the top five 
substances identified for females.
