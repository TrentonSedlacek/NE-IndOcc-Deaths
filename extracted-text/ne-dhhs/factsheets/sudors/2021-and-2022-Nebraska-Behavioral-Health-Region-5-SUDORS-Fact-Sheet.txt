cause of overdose deaths in Region 5,

and 44 years for females. Substance pat -
terns were generally similar across sexes, 
though fentanyl ranked second for males 
and fourth for females, while antidepres -
sants ranked third and anticonvulsants 
fifth for females but did not appear among 
the top five substances for males.
