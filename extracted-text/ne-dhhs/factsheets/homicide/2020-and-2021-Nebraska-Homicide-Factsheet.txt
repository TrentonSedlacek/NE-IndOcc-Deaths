Demographics in Nebraska homicide deaths in 2020 and 2021 
were not reflective of the population demographics. Black and 
Hispanic people died by homicide at higher rates than White 
people given their population size in Nebraska. Other risk 
factors that surpassed the Nebraskan population demographics 
were being male, between the ages of 20-29, and having a high 
school degree level of education or lower. 
The highest percentage of homicide deaths in Nebraska occurred in May to August in 2020 and 2021.
