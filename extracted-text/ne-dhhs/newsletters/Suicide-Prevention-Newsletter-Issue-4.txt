Suicides Among Individuals Aged 55 and Older in Nebraska in 2022
In this quarter’s issue, we’re focusing on s uicide among individuals aged 55 and older. Research has 
shown a potential link between older adults and increased mental health challenges during the 
winter months, 
as shorter days and colder weather can contribute to feelings of isolation and 
depression
. By examining suicide trends in this age group, we aim to provide insights and resources 
that can help address these unique seasonal challenges and support prevention efforts.
