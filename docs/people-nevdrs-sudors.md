## What NEVDRS says about its own suicide counts (dashboard page)

- Residents only, including residents who died out of state (inter-jurisdictional exchange).
- Suicide = X60 to X84, Y87.0, U03. Same as our definition.
- Counts can differ from vital statistics because NEVDRS reclassifies deaths after investigation (a death first coded as undetermined may become a suicide). This is a likely source of the 72 vs 65 construction gap alongside industry assignment, and worth asking about.
- Dashboard suppresses counts of 1 to 5 and reports age-adjusted rates to the 2000 US standard with Census population denominators.
