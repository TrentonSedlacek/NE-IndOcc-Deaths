## What NEVDRS says about its own suicide counts (dashboard page)

- Residents only, including residents who died out of state (inter-jurisdictional exchange).
- Suicide = X60 to X84, Y87.0, U03. Same as our definition.
- Counts can differ from vital statistics because deaths are reclassified after investigation (the page's example: a death not initially reported as a suicide may later be determined to be one). (inference) This and the out-of-state residents above are possible sources of the 72 vs 65 construction gap, alongside industry assignment and age; worth asking about.
- Dashboard suppresses counts of 1 to 5 and reports age-adjusted rates to the 2000 US standard with Census population denominators.
