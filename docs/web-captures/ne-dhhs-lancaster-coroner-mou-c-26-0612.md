MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING BETWEEN THE NEBRASKA DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES AND LANCASTER COUNTY ATTORNEY'S OFFICE

This Memorandum of Understanding ("MOU"), including any addenda and attachments (collectively, "Agreement") is entered into by and between the Nebraska Department of Health and Human Services, Division of Public Health Nebraska Violent Death Reporting System (NeVDRS) and State Unintentional Drug Overdose Reporting System (SUDORS) ("DHHS"), and the Lancaster County Attorney's Office ("Coroner's Office"), (collectively, "Parties").

PURPOSE: The purpose of this Agreement is to define when and how DHHS requests and receives Death Information from County Attorney's Office and the conditions under which DHHS may use Death Information received from County Attorney's Office.

1. DEFINITIONS
1.1. 'Violent death' includes any death by homicide or suicide, and unintentional firearm deaths and deaths of undetermined intent.
1.2. 'Death Information' includes the coroner or medical examiner report; autopsy report; toxicology report; any additional reports produced during the death investigation; information about firearms involved in the incident including the type, caliber, model, and NCIC code; and an investigative summary including circumstantial or causal information related to the incident.
1.3. 'PII' includes any data that can be used to identify, contact, or locate a specific individual either directly or indirectly when combined with other information.

2. TERM & TERMINATION
2.1. TERM. This Agreement is in effect from June 1, 2026 through December 31, 2031.
2.2. TERMINATION. This Agreement may be terminated, in whole or in part, at any time upon mutual written consent, or by either party for any reason upon submission of written notice to the other party at least thirty (30) days prior to the effective date of termination. Either party may also terminate the Agreement to the extent otherwise provided herein. Upon either termination or expiration of this Agreement, the Parties shall confer as to the disposal of any data involved in the Agreement, and agree, in writing as to the manner of method of disposal.

3. EXCHANGE OF FUNDS
3.1. There will be no exchange of money between the Parties to perform the tasks necessary to carry out the purpose of this MOU. Each party will provide their own in-kind contributions, as needed, and will absorb any administration costs incurred through this process.

4. RESPONSIBILITIES
4.1. DHHS shall:
4.1.1. Identify applicable deaths via death certificate information and request the relevant Death Information from the County Attorney's Office.
4.1.2. Only submit data to the Centers for Disease Control (CDC) National Violent Death Reporting System (NDVRS) and SUDORS database in aggregate form that includes no PII.
4.1.3. Securely store Death Information provided by County Attorney's Office on State of Nebraska servers.
4.1.4. Ensure that Death Information is only accessible to the employees necessary to meet the obligations of this Agreement within DHHS.
4.1.5. Ensure that Death Information is not disclosed or distributed beyond the scope of this Agreement unless required by law.
4.2. County Attorney's Office shall:
4.2.1. County Attorney's Office will submit Death Information upon request as described in Addendum A.
4.3. Best Efforts. The parties shall use their best efforts to accomplish their respective responsibilities in a timely and efficient manner. The failure of one party to perform its responsibilities shall not relieve the other party of its responsibilities.

5. ALLOWABLE USES
5.1. DHHS may use Death Information for the following reasons:
5.1.1. To translate surveillance findings into prevention strategies, improve local, state, or national violence prevention.
5.1.2. To collect and maintain complete and comprehensive surveillance data on violent and unintentional drug overdose deaths that occur in Nebraska.
5.1.3. To disclose aggregated and deidentified Death Information to NVDRS, SUDORS, violence prevention partners, and the public.

6. INDEPENDENT AGENCIES
6.1. DHHS and the County Attorney's Office are independent agencies within the State of Nebraska. This Agreement shall not create an employer-employee relationship between the Parties or between any of the employees of one party with the other party. The Agreement does not create a business partnership or joint venture under Nebraska law, or any joint entity as set forth in the Interlocal Cooperation Act, Neb. Rev. Stat. §§ 13-801 et seq.

7. WRITTEN AGREEMENT
7.1. Amendment. This Agreement may be modified only by written amendment, executed by both parties. No alteration or variation of the terms and conditions of this Agreement shall be valid unless made in writing and signed by the Parties.
7.2. Integration. This written Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties, and any prior or contemporaneous representations, promises, or statements by the Parties, which are not incorporated herein, shall not serve to vary or contradict the terms set forth in this Agreement.
7.3. Severability. Should any part, term or provision of this Agreement be determined to be invalid, the remainder of this Agreement shall not be affected, and the same shall continue in full force and effect.
7.4. Survival. All provisions hereof that by their nature are to be performed or complied with following the expiration or termination of this Agreement, shall survive the expiration or termination of this Agreement.

8. COMPLIANCE WITH LAW
8.1. If required by the Interlocal Cooperation Act, the County Attorney's Office shall ensure that this Agreement is properly filed with the Nebraska Auditor of Public Accounts by the applicable deadline, in accordance with Neb. Rev. Stat. § 13-513.

Rev. Stat. §§ 48-1101 to 48-1125.
8.3. The Parties shall comply with all other applicable federal, state, county and municipal laws, ordinances, and rules and regulations in the performance of this Agreement. This may include, but is not limited to, confidentiality requirements for the information being accessed or the data being shared, as may be more fully set forth herein.

9. GOVERNING LAW & VENUE
9.1. Any disputes under this MOU will be bound under the laws of the State of Nebraska. The parties agree that the proper venue for any suit between them regarding this Agreement shall be filed in the District Court of Lancaster County, Nebraska.

10. LIABILITY
10.1. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as an indemnification by one party or the other for liabilities of a party or third parties for property loss or damage or death or personal injury arising out of and during the performance of this Agreement. Any liabilities or claims for property loss or damages or for death or personal injury by a party or its agents, employees, contractors or assigns or by third persons shall be determined according to applicable law.
10.2. Nothing in this Agreement shall relieve either party of any obligation or responsibility imposed upon it by law.

11. RECORDS
11.1. The Parties shall maintain all records related to this Agreement as consistent with any applicable record retention schedules, or any other retention requirement mandated by law.
11.2. Except as specified in this Agreement, DHHS shall not disclose, release, or otherwise grant access to the records covered by this agreement to any third party for any purpose unless required by law. DHHS agrees that access to the data covered by this Agreement shall be limited to those individuals necessary to achieve the purpose stated in this agreement.

12. NO THIRD-PARTY BENEFICIARY RIGHTS

AGREEMENT TERMS
Parties agree to the following:
1. Definitions
a. "Agreement" is this data sharing agreement, including its exhibits, attachments, and amendments.
b. "Applicable Law" means all national, state, and local laws, rules, and regulations that apply to the Parties' activities.
c. "Authorized Users" means a person who is authorized to receive, maintain, process, view, handle, examine, interpreter, or analyze the data and has agreed to the restrictions imposed under this Agreement.
d. "Data" means any communication or record, whether oral, written, electronically stored, electronically transmitted, or an any other form, provided or made available pursuant to this Agreement.
e. "Death Information" means the coroner or medical examiner report; autopsy report; toxicology report; any additional reports produced during the death investigation; information about firearms involved in the incident including the type, caliber, model, and NCIC code; and an investigative summary including circumstantial or causal information related to the incident.
f. "Confidential data" means all data owned, managed, created, or received for or on behalf of DHHS that is protected by Federal or State information, security, privacy, or confidentiality laws, or rules.
g. "Breach" means an impermissible use or disclosure of electronic or non-electronic data by an unauthorized person or for an unauthorized purpose that compromises security or privacy, and the use or disclosure poses a risk of reputational harm, theft of financial information, identity theft, or medical identity theft.
h. "HIPAA" means the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996, including the specific requirements in the "Privacy Rule" or "HIPAA Privacy Rule" and the "Security Rule" or "HIPAA Security Rule" at 45 CFR 160, 162, and 164.
2. Scope
a. This Agreement shall encompass the transmission, use, storage, and disposition of data and data sets listed in Appendix A.
b. Authorized Users
i. NVDRS Epidemiologist I
ii. Injury Surveillance Epidemiologist III
iii. Epidemiology Unit Administrator
c. Authorized Uses
i. Parties may only use the data and data sets listed in Appendix A for the purpose set forth in Section I of this Agreement and cannot otherwise use or disclose the data and data sets unless required by applicable law or authorized by this Agreement.
d. Transmission of Data – Death Information shall be transmitted from County Attorney's Office to DHHS using the transmission methods listed below as requested.
i. Electronically via Secure E-mail, via postal mail, or by upload via Secure FTP site.
ii. Parties shall use appropriate technical, physical, and administrative safeguards for the secure transmission of confidential data as defined HIPAA and related United States Department of Health and Human Services guidance.
e. Secure Data Storage
i. Parties shall use appropriate technical, physical, and administrative safeguards for the secured storage of confidential data as defined under HIPAA and related United States Department of Health and Human Services guidance.
3. Data Ownership
a. DHHS shall retain all ownership interests in data and data sets provided by DHHS, as indicated in Appendix A.
b. County Attorney's Office shall retain all ownership interests in the data provided by County Attorney's Office, as indicated in Appendix A.
c. Neither Party shall obtain any ownership interest in the data or data sets provided by the other Party without explicit written consent.
4. Confidentiality
a. The Parties acknowledge that applicable laws are in place to protect client confidentiality and privacy. Any sharing or disclosing of legally protected information between the parties to this Agreement shall fully comply with applicable laws.
5. Breach
a. Any acquisition, access, use, disclosure, or loss of data, other than as permitted by this Agreement, shall be presumed to be a breach unless the Party demonstrates, based on a risk assessment, that there is a low probability that the data has been compromised.
b. Breaching party shall, at breaching Party's expense, cooperate fully with DHHS in investigating, mitigating, and issuing notifications, as directed by DHHS, for any breach.
c. Initial Breach Notice
i. Breaching party agrees to notify DHHS of the breach within five (5) business days of discovery. Notification shall be provided to the designated individual, as listed above.
ii. Notification will include the following information about the breach: 1. Date and time of incident; 2. Date and time the incident was discovered; 3. Description of the incident and the data involved; 4. Potential number of records involved; 5. Location the incident occurred; 6. The information system involved in the incident; and 7. Any other information reasonably related to the incident.
d. If the breaching Party opens an investigation on the cause of the breach, the Party agrees to send weekly status updates to the designated individual, as listed above.
e. When the investigation has been completed, breaching Party will communicate the results of the investigation to the designated individual, as listed above.
f. If the investigation and subsequent risk assessment determines the incident did not raise to the level of a breach, based on a low probability of compromise, the breaching Party will provide the following: i. Investigation Summary Report; and ii. Completed Risk Assessment.
g. If the investigation and subsequent risk assessment result in the finding of a confirmed breach, the breaching Party will: i. Provide the DHHS designated individual with the following: 1. Date the breach occurred; 2. Date the Party discovered the breach; 3. Description of the breach; 4. Description of the data involved; 5. Identification of individuals affected by the breach; 6. Mitigation steps the Party has taken to prevent the type of breach from occurring again; and 7. Any reasonably available, pertinent information, documents or reports related to the breach; ii. Notify required regulatory authorities; and iii. Sending DHHS approved breach notification letters to all affected individuals.
h. If DHHS determines there has been an incident or breach, DHHS may, in its sole discretion, immediately and unilaterally terminate this Agreement upon notice to County Attorney's Office. The privacy and security requirements including restrictions on transmission, secure data storage, data ownership, and data disposal provisions of this Agreement shall survive the termination of this Agreement.
6. Data Disposal
a. Upon termination of this Agreement, Parties will promptly and securely destroy the data and data set listed in Appendix A, using industry-accepted methods. The disposing Parties shall sign an Attestation of Destruction, attached as Appendix B, attesting to the same. The terms of the Agreement shall remain effective in their entirety until DHHS receives the signed statement attesting to destruction.
