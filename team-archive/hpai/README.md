# HPAI (team archive)

Moved from `HPAI/` on 2026-09-28. This folder is unrelated to the industry and occupation deaths project and is parked here only to keep the team material together. It holds the occupational health team's spring 2024 response material on highly pathogenic avian influenza A(H5N1) in Nebraska dairy cattle and poultry: a draft DHHS Health Alert Network notice (April 17, 2024), CDC-derived worker PPE and farmer guidance documents, a list of USDA data sources (Farm Labor Survey and others), a slide deck of county farm production maps, an R script plotting the national USDA APHIS epi curve of confirmed cattle herds from March 2024 (its input CSVs are not in the repo), a USDA NASS county livestock inventory extract, a list of BLS QCEW series IDs for Nebraska county employment in agricultural NAICS codes (112111, 112112, 11212, 1122, 1123), and a workbook of 2022 Nebraska employment and farm counts for beef, feedlot, dairy, hog, and poultry industries by county.
